About Plemmo

Built for the businesses
that built Britain.

Plemmo exists for one reason: to give independent UK businesses the same quality of service, pricing and support that the big chains take for granted. We're a growth toolkit, a sounding board, and on most days, the team that actually answers the phone.

Our story

Why we started Plemmo.

We spent years watching brilliant local businesses — corner shops, takeaways, salons, cafés, garages — get squeezed by providers who didn't care, contracts they didn't understand, and fees that quietly grew every quarter.

So we did something about it. We pulled together the services every business needs — payments, EPOS, funding, signage, utilities, support — and built a single team to handle the lot. No call centres reading scripts. No "account manager" who changes every month. Just a team that knows your name, your business, and your numbers.

That's Plemmo.

Who we work with

Independent retailers, takeaways, restaurants, cafés, salons, barbers, garages, convenience stores, grocers, butchers, professional services — the businesses that make British high streets worth walking down.

If you run a business and you're tired of being treated like a number, we're for you.

What makes us different

We're not a provider — we're your advocate.

01

We sit on your side of the table

Most companies sell you their product. We find the right product for you, from a panel of trusted UK partners. If a deal doesn't make sense for your business, we'll tell you.

02

We do the boring stuff

Comparing rates. Reading the small print. Chasing switchovers. Negotiating contracts. It's tedious, and it costs business owners thousands a year. We handle it — and we don't charge you to do it.

Honesty about commissions. Honesty about fit. Honesty about whether a product is even right for you. That honesty is the whole point.
