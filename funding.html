Funding options we introduce

Four ways to fund your next move.

📈

Unsecured Business Loans

Fixed-term funding for clear, planned business purposes — refits, equipment, expansion, working capital.

  • Typically £10,000 to £500,000+
  • Fixed monthly repayments, set by the lender
  • Best when you know exactly what the money's for
💳

Merchant Cash Advance

An advance against your future card sales. The lender takes a small percentage of each card transaction until it's repaid — so when sales are quiet, repayments are too.

  • Repayments scale with daily card sales
  • No fixed monthly burden
  • Brilliant for hospitality and retail with seasonal swings
📊

Revenue-Based Finance

Like an MCA, but tied to overall business revenue rather than just card transactions. Flexible, performance-linked, and growth-friendly.

  • Repayments flex with your top line
  • Suited to ambitious, growing businesses
🛠️

Credit Lines & Asset Finance

Revolving credit you can dip in and out of, or purpose-built finance for specific kit — vehicles, equipment, machinery.

  • Draw down what you need, when you need it
  • Asset finance: spread the cost of essential equipment
